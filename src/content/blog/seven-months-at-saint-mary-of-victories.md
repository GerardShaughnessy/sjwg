---
title: 'Seven months at Saint Mary of Victories'
description: 'What Guild members have done at the parish so far, in plain terms.'
pubDate: 2026-08-20
author: 'Daniel Kovach, secretary'
---

In the Guild's first seven months at Saint Mary of Victories, members have cleared fallen foliage from the parking lot, installed an inline electric commercial coffee system, repaired a leaking window, installed a new toilet, and handled routine inspections, general maintenance, and diagnostic work at no charge.

The parish has saved more than $5,000 in discounted work over that time.

The window was the one that stuck with me. It had leaked for years and the sill was soft all the way through. Two of us spent a Saturday on it. The pastor kept bringing coffee out. That is the whole Guild in one afternoon.

Photos of the finished work are coming.
