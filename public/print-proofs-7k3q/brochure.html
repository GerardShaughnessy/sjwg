Why a guild

A local Catholic economy.

  • Homeowners and parishes: a tradesman who values honest work and the dignity of labor.
  • Tradesmen: customers who want to support Catholic-owned businesses.
  • Community: charity work, educational resources, and mutual aid.

“We greatly wish, for the salvation of the people, that … these guilds may be generally restored … to help the honest workman, to protect and guard his children and family, and to promote in them piety, Christian knowledge, and a moral life.”

Pope Leo XIII, Humanum Genus, 1884
Tradesmen

Become a Guild brother.

For practicing Catholic men in the skilled trades. Members get:

  • Referral network and directory listing

  • Masses, Holy Hours, and retreats for the trades
  • Business ethics and networking
